Pore-C in-silico restriction-enzyme explorer

Upload a genome FASTA + a centromere BED and explore how any NEB restriction enzyme would digest it — cut density, fragment (monomer) lengths, and centromere-vs-arm balance, per chromosome. Everything runs in your browser; no data is uploaded. Based on the Henderson-lab benchmark.

By Jacob Gonzalez Isa (jg2070@cam.ac.uk). Acknowledgements: Katie Jenike (kj436@cam.ac.uk) — who designed the cut-density track — and Hanwen Cao (hc617@cam.ac.uk).

Balance map

x = 0 means equal cutting in centromere & arms. Left = over-cuts centromere; right = under-cuts it. Marker colour = overall cut density (cuts/kb, log scale) — a third dimension beyond the axes. Hover for the enzyme.

Cut-density track

The overview auto-coarsens for speed; the chosen bin size is the finest used — zoom in (drag or box-select) to reach it. Double-click to reset.

Predicted monomer (fragment) lengths

Distance between cuts ≈ Pore-C monomer length. Nanopore sweet spot ~0.8–1.4 kb (dotted lines).
